What the numbers mean
Ride hours and miles
Moving time and distance added up from every ride (road, indoor, gravel, MTB, track) by calendar week (Monday to Sunday), month and year. Strength and other workouts are left out.
Missing rides
Strava doesn't let intervals.icu pass on activities that reached it only through Strava. To count, a ride has to come into intervals.icu from Garmin, Wahoo, Coros, Zwift or another direct link, or be uploaded there.
Fitness
intervals.icu's chronic training load (CTL): roughly a 6-week average of daily training load. Higher means more fit.
Form
Fitness minus fatigue (TSB). About −10 to −30 is productive training (green); below −30 is high fatigue risk (red); above +5 is fresh.
eFTP
intervals.icu's estimated functional threshold power from recent rides, in watts and per kg of body weight.
Power PR
A best effort in the last 42 days that matches or beats the rider's best for that duration over the past year. Only riders with a power meter or smart trainer have power numbers.
